Post-Sales Outreach

One place for the post-sales team to own email to existing clients: who to reach, what material fits them, what went out, and how close each account is to its monthly cap. It replaces ad-hoc CEO emails with owned, governed outreach, and extends reach to the leaders we don't sell to yet: CHRO, CMO, CFO.
