Available now

What works today

Access and ownership

  • Each person sees only the accounts they own; the post-sales lead sees every account.
  • The lead adds and removes account owners from one screen.
  • Sign-in with a Lyzr Microsoft account is ready to switch on.

Knowing who to reach

  • An accounts dashboard with the most neglected accounts first.
  • My targets: win back churned accounts, accounts going quiet, renewals coming up, prospects to warm up.
  • Team coverage shows how well each person's accounts are being contacted, and which have no owner.

Understanding an account

  • Engaged stakeholders and the leadership team (CHRO, CMO, CFO) side by side.
  • The last email sent, who sent it, and this month's count against the limit.
  • Customer status, opt-outs, and cross-sell suggestions for each leader.

Finding material

  • Search the collateral library in plain English; Claude works out the product, role and type of material.
  • "Find collateral" on any contact brings up material that fits their role and the account's products.
